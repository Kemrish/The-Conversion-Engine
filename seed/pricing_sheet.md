# Tenacious Consulting and Outsourcing — Pricing Sheet (Public Tiers)

## Talent Outsourcing (Managed Teams)

| Tier | Team Size | Monthly Rate | ACV Range | Segment Fit |
|------|-----------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| Starter | 2–3 engineers | $18K–$28K/mo | $216K–$336K | Segment 1 |
| Growth | 4–6 engineers | $32K–$54K/mo | $384K–$648K | Segment 1, 2 |
| Scale | 7–12 engineers | $56K–$96K/mo | $672K–$1.15M | Segment 2, 3 |

**Standard engagement duration:** 6–24 months  
**Minimum commitment:** 6 months  
**Notice period:** 60 days

### What's included
- Dedicated engineers operating under Tenacious management
- Weekly delivery reporting to client PM
- Tenacious-managed hiring, HR, equipment
- Performance SLA: defined sprint velocity targets (specifics in contract)
- Ramp period: 2–4 weeks (not billed at full rate)

### What requires human escalation
- Specific engineer CVs or background check results
- Custom SLA terms
- Sub-6-month engagements
- Dedicated team lead pricing
- Equity or hybrid pricing structures

## Pricing Language Rules (from style_guide.md)
- Quote ranges, never point estimates: "typically $18K–$28K per month"
- Always say "based on our public pricing tiers"
- Never negotiate via email or SMS
- If prospect asks for specific pricing below these bands: route to human
- Discovery call purpose is scoping, not closing — pricing is discussed after scoping

## Competitor Positioning (Price)
Tenacious sits below top-tier US consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG tech practices: $200K–$500K/month) and above low-cost offshore mills ($8K–$15K/month). The value proposition is: quality of a mid-market US firm at 40–60% of the cost, with Tenacious management accountability.

Do not quote competitor prices directly. Frame as: "top-quartile B2B tech companies in your sector typically pay X for equivalent capacity."
