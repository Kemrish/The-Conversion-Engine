# Cold Email Sequence — Tenacious Consulting and Outsourcing

## Sequence Rules
- Day 0: First touch (research-led, signal-grounded)
- Day 4: Follow-up (new data point from brief)
- Day 10: Re-engagement (different angle)
- Day 21: Final close
- No contact after Day 21 unless prospect re-engages
