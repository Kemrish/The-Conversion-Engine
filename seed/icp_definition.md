# Tenacious Consulting and Outsourcing — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Segment 1: Recently-Funded Series A/B Startups
**Description:** Companies that closed a $5–30M round in the last 6 months. Typically 15–80 people. Hiring velocity outstripping recruiting capacity.

**Qualifying signals:**
- Funding announcement (Series A or B) within last 180 days
- $5M–$30M raised
- 15–80 employees
- 3+ open engineering roles
- No recent layoff event (last 120 days)

**Disqualifying signals:**
- Raised > $30M (mid-market pitch instead)
- Laid off > 10% of staff in last 120 days (Segment 2 pitch instead)
- Fewer than 3 open engineering roles (no hiring urgency)
- Pre-revenue or idea stage

**Why they buy:** Need to scale engineering output faster than in-house hiring can support. Budget is fresh and runway is the clock.

**Pitch angle (AI readiness ≥ 2):** "Scale your AI team faster than in-house hiring can support — we provide dedicated engineers who are already production-ready."

**Pitch angle (AI readiness 0–1):** "Stand up your first AI function with a dedicated squad while your core product team stays focused."

---

## Segment 2: Mid-Market Platforms Restructuring Cost
**Description:** Public or late-stage companies (200–2,000 people) under pressure to cut burn without cutting output. Often post-layoff or post-restructure.

**Qualifying signals:**
- 200–2,000 employees
- Layoff event in last 120 days OR public cost-cutting announcement
- Active engineering roles despite layoff (signal: replacing high-cost with offshore)
- Revenue > $10M (can afford engagement)

**Disqualifying signals:**
- < 200 employees (Segment 1 pitch instead)
- No cost-cutting signal
- Fully remote-first with strong offshore bench already

**Why they buy:** Replace higher-cost roles with offshore equivalents; keep delivery capacity; quiet signal of operational discipline to the board.

**Pitch angle:** "Maintain your engineering output at 60% of the previous cost — with dedicated teams under Tenacious management."

---

## Segment 3: Engineering-Leadership Transitions
**Description:** Companies with a new CTO or VP Engineering appointed in the last 90 days.

**Qualifying signals:**
- New CTO or VP Engineering within last 90 days (LinkedIn / Crunchbase / press releases)
- Company has 30–500 employees
- Active engineering hiring OR recent restructure

**Disqualifying signals:**
- Leadership change > 90 days ago (window is closing)
- Internal promotion with no prior offshore evaluation (lower conversion)
- < 20 employees (too early)

**Why they buy:** New leaders routinely reassess vendor contracts and offshore mix in their first 6 months. This is a narrow but high-conversion window.

**Pitch angle:** "New engineering leaders typically reassess offshore mix in the first 90 days — here's what top-quartile teams in your sector look like."

---

## Segment 4: Specialized Capability Gaps
**Description:** Companies attempting a specific build — ML platform migration, agentic systems, data contracts — where in-house skills do not match the need.

**Qualifying signals:**
- AI maturity score ≥ 2 (required gate)
- Open roles: ML engineer, Applied Scientist, LLM engineer, AI Product Manager, Data Platform Engineer
- No Head of AI or equivalent (gap exists)
- Tech stack includes: Python, PyTorch, dbt, Snowflake, Databricks, Ray, or vLLM
- Specific AI initiative mentioned in press, blog, or job descriptions

**Disqualifying signals:**
- AI maturity score < 2 (wrong pitch)
- Already has a full AI team (no gap to fill)
- < 20 engineers total (too small for project consulting)

**Why they buy:** Project-based consulting, not outsourcing. Higher margin, shorter commitment, portfolio value.

**Pitch angle:** "We've seen three companies in your sector fail this exact migration in the last 12 months — here's the gap we see between your current setup and what the build requires."

## Segment Priority Matrix

| Signal Combination | Primary Segment | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Recent Series A/B + 3+ eng roles + no layoff | Segment 1 | High |
| Layoff + still hiring + 200+ employees | Segment 2 | High |
| New CTO/VP Eng < 90 days | Segment 3 | High |
| AI maturity ≥ 2 + specific AI gap | Segment 4 | High |
| Recent funding + layoff | Segment 2 (over Segment 1) | Medium |
| New leader + recent funding | Segment 3 (primary) + Segment 1 note | Medium |
| Multiple signals present | Route to Segment 3 if leader change present | Medium |

## Global Disqualifiers (any segment)
- Government / public sector (sales cycle too long)
- Healthcare or financial services without tech focus
- < 10 employees total
- Founded < 6 months ago (too early)
- Primary market outside North America + Europe + East Africa
- Previous negative Tenacious interaction (check CRM)
